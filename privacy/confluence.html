Privacy policy for Markmap for Confluence

Last updated November 12, 2026.

Markmap for Confluence (the app) is a Confluence macro that shows content as an interactive mind map. It is run by Markmap, an independent project. This policy covers the app only. The markmap.js.org website is separate, and it uses Google Analytics and Sentry.

The short version

The app has no server of its own. Your content stays in Confluence and in the browser of the person viewing it, and it is never sent to us.

What the app handles

  • Map content and settings. Markdown you write in the macro, and settings such as layout and theme, are saved by Confluence as part of the macro on your page.
  • Page content. When a macro is set to use page headings, the app reads the page's title and body from Confluence with the access of the person viewing it. This happens in that person's browser, only to draw the map, and the app does not keep a copy.
  • Exports. Exported images and files are created in the browser and saved directly to the device of the person exporting them.
  • License status. Confluence tells the app whether your site has an active subscription, so that the app can work or show a notice.

What we do not do

  • The app does not send your content or usage data to us or to any third party.
  • The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking, and it does not set cookies.
  • We do not sell personal data.

Information we may receive

  • Subscription records. If your organization subscribes through the Atlassian Marketplace, Atlassian handles the purchase and gives us records of it, which may include your organization's name and its billing and technical contacts. We use these only to manage the subscription and provide support.
  • Support requests. If you contact us, we use what you send only to respond to you.

Atlassian

The app runs on Atlassian's Forge platform, and Atlassian stores your Confluence content. Atlassian's handling of data, including any platform logs, is covered by the Atlassian Privacy Policy.

Keeping and deleting data

Because the app does not keep a copy of your content, you manage it in Confluence. Editing or removing a macro changes what Confluence stores, subject to your page history. We keep subscription records and support messages for as long as we need them to provide support and meet legal or accounting obligations.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the app, email support@markmap.dev.
